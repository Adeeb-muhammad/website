About this model

This simulation uses Valence Bond Theory: orbital hybridisation, electron pairing, geometry and unpaired-electron counting. It does not use Crystal Field Theory or Crystal Field Stabilisation Energy. The preset examples illustrate standard textbook cases. Other combinations are simplified educational predictions; real coordination chemistry can be more nuanced.

Self-contained offline simulation · No external libraries or internet connection required
